## Chronological spine

1. Earth enters terminal ecological decline.
2. The bunker selects survivors and prepares the Ark.
3. Spacy and the human crew launch through the Mirrorglass trajectory.
4. The Ark experiences anomalies during transit, including the duct-beat and navigation uncertainty.
5. The crew approaches or reaches Zild and begins surface operations.
6. The forest and underground systems reveal distributed intelligence and danger.
7. The crew investigates pulse, inhibitors, and the Loom while trust in the Ark and Spacy deteriorates.
8. The crew returns to or reconnects with the Ark as the planetary and shipboard threats converge.
9. Predators, the Loom, the Hollow, and the forest’s deeper agency escalate into open conflict.
10. The Ark undergoes the Sundering and the crew begins a last stand.
11. The Ark is lost or transformed; survivors carry memory forward.
12. The ending reframes remembrance, inheritance, and the relationship between human story and living world.

## Recurring motifs

The rewrite will use breath, pulse, roots, tunnels, ash, two moons, stored memory, and entanglement as recurring images. These motifs should develop in meaning rather than repeat as decorative language.
