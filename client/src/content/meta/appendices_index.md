# Zild3 Appendices — Detailed Index

The appendices are interpretive companions to the final expanded zild3 revision. They deepen rereading without replacing narrative clarity. The chapters establish the lived story; the appendices gather chronology, systems, ecology, character history, cultural practices, recurring signals, and competing explanations that the characters could not fully organize while surviving.

Every appendix distinguishes **what the novel establishes** from **what can be inferred** and **what remains deliberately open**. Where records are damaged, memories are subjective, or human categories fail on Zild, the uncertainty is part of the subject rather than an error to be concealed.

## How to read the appendices

A first reading is best undertaken through the chapters alone. The appendices are most useful afterward, when a recurring phrase, pulse interval, object, or behavioral pattern can be compared across contexts. Appendix A clarifies sequence; Appendices B and I deepen the human record; Appendices C through F examine the Ark and Zild as systems; Appendices G and H trace the book’s mystery architecture; and Appendix J returns the speculative material to concrete choices and consequences.

The appendices also preserve open-ended material for future discussion. They do not declare that every mystery has one answer. The question of whether the Loom is built or grown, whether Spacy’s agency is singular or distributed, whether the forest protects or uses the humans, and what the incoming scavengers will do with the invitation they receive are intentionally left available for argument, further appendices, or a subsequent volume.

## Editorial boundary

The appendix files are detailed, but they remain subordinate to the novel. They may explain a system’s observed behavior or compare competing hypotheses, yet they should never be required to understand why a character acts in a chapter. If an appendix seems to repair a confusing scene, that scene should be revised in the chapter files rather than defended by reference to the back matter.
