# Easter-Egg and Appendix Ledger

This ledger separates **reader-facing seeds** from **appendix-worthy questions**. Easter eggs should reward attentive rereading without making the main plot dependent on recognition. Appendix questions should remain suggestive in the chapters and receive fuller treatment only in supporting material.

|---|---|---|---|

## Rules for using the ledger

An Easter egg must be attached to a concrete action, sensory detail, object, or line of dialogue. It may not appear as an unexplained authorial wink. An appendix question must remain answerable in principle but need not be answered in the main novel. If a question is essential to a character’s immediate decision, the chapter must provide enough information for that decision; the appendix cannot substitute for narrative clarity.

The final pass will check that each planted detail either recurs, gains a new interpretation, or is intentionally left as atmosphere. Details that never matter and cannot support the world or theme will be removed rather than multiplied.
